Hello Hands privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Hello Hands is a British Sign Language learning app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is operated by Tokár Ádám, sole proprietor, Hungary ("we").

The short version

Hello Hands collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

What stays on your devices

  • Your learned signs, review schedule, streak, saved signs, onboarding answers and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Your streak and Pro status are also sent from your iPhone to your own paired Apple Watch so the Watch app can show them. Deleting the app deletes this data, and Settings, Reset my progress deletes it at any time.
  • Mirror practice shows your front camera on screen next to the sign video. The camera picture is never recorded, saved or uploaded. You can say no to camera access and still use every other feature.
  • The daily phrase reminder and the trial reminder are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Hello Hands Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The app only checks with Apple whether Pro is active.

Emails you send us

If you email us to report a sign or ask for help, we use your email only to reply and to fix the sign, and we delete it when the matter is closed.

Children

Hello Hands does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
