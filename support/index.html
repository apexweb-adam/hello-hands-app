Hello Hands support

Email info@apexweb.hu and we reply within two working days.

A sign looks wrong, or my area signs it differently

Open the sign in the app and tap Report a problem with this sign. Regional signs are welcome, and we review every report.

Cancel or change my subscription

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Hello Hands. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Restore Pro on a new phone

In the app open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

The Watch app shows no streak

Open Hello Hands on your iPhone once with your Watch nearby. The streak syncs from the phone.
