Subscriptions

Hello Hands Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, weekly or yearly. A free trial, when offered, turns into a paid subscription at the end of the trial unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

What Hello Hands is

Hello Hands teaches everyday British Sign Language signs. It is not a qualification and does not replace a qualified BSL interpreter for medical, legal or official situations. Sign videos are licensed from their signers and may not be copied out of the app.
